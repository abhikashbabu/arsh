Orthopaedic Care in Gaya

Choosing an Orthopaedic Hospital in Gaya

Finding the right orthopaedic doctor in Gaya matters more once pain starts affecting how you walk, work or sleep. ARSH Super Speciality Hospital brings joint, bone and spine care to Gaya so patients from the city and the wider Magadh region don't need to travel to Patna or beyond for an accurate diagnosis and a clear treatment plan.

Every consultation starts with understanding how the condition affects daily life, followed by clinical examination and imaging where required. Many orthopaedic problems — including early arthritis, minor ligament injuries and mechanical back pain — respond well to non-surgical treatment such as physiotherapy, activity modification and medication. Surgery is discussed only when it's genuinely the more effective option, and explained clearly before any decision is made.

For patients in and around Gaya who do need surgery — joint replacement, fracture fixation or arthroscopy — recovery is planned from day one, with follow-up guidance to support a safe return to normal activity.

Patient questions

Orthopaedic Care FAQs — ARSH Hospital, Gaya

For a diagnosis and personalised advice, please consult a qualified orthopaedic specialist.

When should I consult an orthopaedic doctor?

Consult a specialist for persistent joint or bone pain, swelling, restricted movement, recurring injuries, deformity or difficulty performing daily activities.

Do all joint problems require surgery?

No. Depending on the diagnosis, treatment may include activity modification, medication, physiotherapy, injections or surgery when clinically appropriate.

What should I bring to my appointment?

Bring previous prescriptions, scans, reports, medication details and a brief history of your symptoms or injury.

How can I book an appointment?

Use the appointment form below or contact the hospital directly to confirm doctor availability and appointment timings.
