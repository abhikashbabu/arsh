International Patients

Serving patients from India and around the world

ARSH Super Speciality Hospital was established in the heart of Bodhgaya, Bihar, with service and excellence as its founding principles. As Bodhgaya is a major destination for travellers from South-East Asia and beyond, thousands of visitors pass through the region every month — and our hospital is built to care for them with the same standard we offer every patient.

Every living soul is welcomed and treated equally here, with world-class services designed to match and exceed expectations. Multiple departments operate under a single roof, so international patients can complete every test and procedure without unnecessary hassle. Our diagnostic labs provide accurate, precise findings, and our doctors, medical teams and support staff are highly qualified and dedicated to their work.
